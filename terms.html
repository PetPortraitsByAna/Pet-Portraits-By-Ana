Terms & Conditions

Copyright

I retain full copyright of all artwork created, including mock-ups, finished pieces and prints. I reserve the right to display commissioned portraits on my website and social media as part of my portfolio. If you would prefer your artwork not to be displayed publicly, please let me know before I begin, and I will respect your wishes.

Payment

Please do not make a payment before contacting me. Once we agree on the details of your order, you will receive payment instructions based on your preferred payment method.

To commission a portrait, full payment is required before the artwork is shipped.

Most orders are dispatched within 1–3 business days after the portrait is completed. Please note that waiting times can be up to 3 months, so I kindly recommend contacting me in advance to ensure your portrait can be completed and delivered in the time you need.

Shipping

All orders are securely shipped with a tracking number to the address you provide. Depending on your location, delivery typically takes around 1–3 weeks.
If your order is a gift, it can also be shipped directly to the recipient.

Waiting Time

Each portrait typically takes around 1–2 weeks to complete. However, as I often work with a client waiting list, waiting time depends on the time of year and my current schedule. I will always provide an estimated completion date when you enquire.

If you have a specific deadline, such as a birthday or special occasion, I will do my best to accommodate your request. To ensure your portrait arrives on time, I recommend placing your order as early as possible.

Changes of finished portrait

Once your portrait is complete, I’ll send you a photo of the finished artwork. If you’d like any small changes or refinements, just let me know. I’ll do my best to make sure you’re truly happy with the final result.
If you wish, I can include your pet’s name in the artwork as a meaningful and personal detail.

Damage in Transit

My portraits are carefully and securely packaged to ensure they arrive safely. However, in the unlikely event that your artwork is damaged during transit, please contact me as soon as possible and include a photo of the damage. Depending on the extent, the artwork must be returned to me and I will create a new portrait for you at no additional cost.

Privacy Policy

I will not sell, share or distribute your personal information to third parties.
