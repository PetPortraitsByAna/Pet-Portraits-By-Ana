1. How long does it take to create a portrait?

The time required to complete your custom artwork depends on its size and complexity of your drawing. On average, a small portrait takes around 5 days to complete, while larger pieces may take two weeks or more.

In addition to production time, there is usually a waiting list of approximately 1 month. Upon enquiry, I will provide an estimated completion time for your portrait, including delivery, which may take up to 14 working days depending on your location worldwide.

If you need your portrait for a specific date, please contact me to check availability. I always recommend reaching out as early as possible.

2. What materials do you use?

Your custom portrait is created using high-quality colored pencils with excellent lightfastness, along with professional-grade markers and paper, ensuring your artwork remains vibrant and beautiful for many years to come.

I use Faber Castell Polychromos 120pcs and Prismacolor colored pencils, Fabriano Bristol Board paper, various alcohol markers, felt pens, rapidographs, white pens, white acrylic, glitter...

3. Can I see the progress of the drawing?

Yes, absolutely. If you'd like, I can send you progress photos throughout the creation process so you can follow along and make sure everything looks just as you imagined.

Once the artwork is finished, I can also share a timelapse video of the drawing process upon request.

4. How many pictures of my pet do you need?

I only require one clear photo to create your portrait, but feel free to send as many as you like. If you're unsure which one would work best, I'll be happy to help you choose the perfect reference.

5. Can you use a low-quality photo to draw my pet?

Yes, I can work with lower-quality photos. However, clear and sharp images are always preferred, as they allow me to capture more detail.

That said, I understand this isn't always possible, especially if your beloved pet has passed and only older photos are available or less detailed images.

Please don't worry, we can still create a beautiful and meaningful portrait.

6. Can you add, remove or change details in the portrait?

Yes, absolutely! Sometimes you may want to make adjustments such as removing a collar, adding a favorite toy, refining colors etc. If you have any specific requests, please share a description or reference photos so I can understand your vision clearly.

For more complex additions, an additional fee may apply. It is also possible to combine multiple pets from different photos into one portrait.

7. Do you ship internationally?

Yes, I ship worldwide. No matter where you're located, your portrait can be delivered to you.

8. Can I track my order?

Yes, of course. Once your order has been dispatched, you will receive an email with your tracking number so you can follow its progress.

9. Do I have to pay shipping costs?

No, you don't have to. Shipping is included in the price, regardless of your location. Whether within the EU or internationally, no additional delivery charges apply.

10. How will I receive the portrait?

Your portrait will be delivered straight to your home, it may also arrive at your local post office for you to pick up.

For safer transport, drawings are carefully rolled and shipped in protective tubes. Once received, you can frame your artwork in any way you like.

11. How do I take care of my artwork?

Your artwork has been created using high-quality materials with excellent lightfastness, meaning your portrait will remain vibrant and beautiful for many years to come. However, there are a few things to keep in mind:

-Keep your artwork out of direct sunlight. Prolonged exposure to strong light or heat may cause the paper to become brittle and the colors to fade over time.

-Avoid pressing or rubbing the surface with your fingers. Although the colored pencils and markers do not easily smudge, applying pressure may take some paint off.

-Framing your artwork is highly recommended - not only does it enhance its appearance, but it also helps protect it and ensures long-term durability.
