1

Pick a photo

A high-resolution photo is always best, as it helps me create the most detailed and accurate portrait possible. You’re also very welcome to send a few different photos and we will choose the best one together.

If you'd like multiple pets in one portrait but don’t have a photo of them all together, that’s absolutely okay! Just send me their individual photos, and I’ll bring them together into one piece.

2

Choose your background

I love adding a soft, colorful watercolor background, as it brings the portrait to life and adds an artistic touch. You’re welcome to request a specific color palette to match the mood you have in mind.

If you’d prefer a more detailed or custom background (specific environments or scenes), this can also be arranged depending on the complexity, an additional fee may apply.

Of course, if you’d rather keep the background minimal, I’ll leave it clean so your pet can truly stand out.

4

Payment

To reserve your place, a 20% deposit is required once details are confirmed. The rest of the amount will be paid upon completion after I show you the final result and make sure you are satisfied.

Your artwork is shipped after payment. You can also pay in full upfront.

Payments can be done through PayPal, WesternUnion, or Moneygram.
