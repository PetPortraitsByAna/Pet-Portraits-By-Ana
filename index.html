I ’ M   A N D R I A N A

I’m a young artist from Serbia and a university
student with a lifelong love for art and animals.

I believe our pets leave paw prints on our hearts,
and every portrait I create is meant to preserve
those precious memories forever.

I draw every portrait entirely by hand, with
countless hours dedicated to carefully capturing
each pet’s unique expression, personality, and
spirit.

My mission is to bring smiles, happy tears, and
lasting memories for the people who love their
pets like family.
